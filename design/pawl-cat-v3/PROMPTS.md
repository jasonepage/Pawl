# Pawl v3 style-frame prompt set

Tool: built-in image generation. The three files in `references/` were supplied as
visual references for character, palette, and mood. They were not edit targets.

## Character model candidate 1

> Establish one authoritative Pawl character model on a neutral warm-ivory production
> sheet. Show the exact same substantial adult gray-brown tabby in front seated,
> three-quarter seated, side standing, curled sleeping, and neutral face close-up views.
> Lock the broad mature head, low olive eyes, pink-brown nose, pale muzzle, forehead M,
> cheek stripes, dorsal stripe, sturdy body, and banded thick tail across every view.
> Use restrained soft editorial-storybook rendering under flat studio light. Include a
> separate front and side turnaround of one modest brass key, approximately the width of
> Pawl's two front paws. No kitten proportions, oversized eyes, accessories, scenery,
> dynamic poses, UI, logo, or extra props.

## Shared base

> Create a high-fidelity, edge-to-edge portrait iPhone style frame for Pawl. Use a
> near-black pine canvas, deep-forest bordered cards, warm-ivory text, native iOS
> typography, conventional controls, and a quiet tab bar. Pawl is a substantial adult
> gray-brown tabby with a broad mature face, stable dark markings, olive half-lidded
> eyes, a pink-brown nose, and long pale whiskers. Render the cat in a restrained soft
> editorial-storybook style. Functional UI must occupy at least 70% of an operational
> screen. The golden physical key may appear once in an illustration only. No key-shaped
> controls, decorative key icons, oversized mascot, kitten proportions, glassmorphism,
> neon, gamification, paw-print pattern, device frame, hands, watermark, or extra copy.

## Home

> Design the progress and support dashboard. Make the `44 days on track` progress ring
> the hero; use Pawl only as a tiny header portrait. Include protection status,
> `I'm tempted`, the current week, and a blocked-items summary. Home is active. Include
> no key, no unlock button, and no access-control action.

## Unlock: blocked

> Design the blocked state of the Unlock tab. Show `PROTECTED`, `Apps are blocked`, a
> short consequence statement, a compact illustration card of Pawl asleep over the
> key, the wait/open duration summary, and a text-only `Tap to unlock` action. Unlock is
> active; Home is inactive.

## Unlock: cooling off

> Design the cooling-off state of the Unlock tab. Make a large monospaced countdown and
> thin amber progress line the hero. Put awake, patient Pawl with a small hourglass and
> the key in a compact supporting card. The only action is `Cancel: keep apps locked`.
> Unlock remains active.

## Unlock: open

> Design the open state of the Unlock tab. Keep the large monospaced re-lock countdown
> as the hero and return progress/action color to moss green. Put calm, alert Pawl and
> the key in a compact supporting card. The sole action is `Lock now`. The mood is
> controlled, not celebratory. Unlock remains active.
