What's in the kit

A security key

A USB-C FIDO2 key, the kind Pawl needs to unlock. It needs no app and no account. Pawl remembers this key and only this key.

Two Pawl Pro codes

Pro adds scheduled sets, which block a different group of apps and sites during the hours you pick, and the full weekly report. Codes are redeemed in Apple's App Store. Use each one by the date printed on the card.

A setup card

Short steps to install Pawl, pick what to block, and pair the key. It also lists good places to keep the key, like with someone across town or in a time lock box.

After setup, the key goes somewhere hard to reach. That can be with you. Whoever holds the key decides when Pawl can loosen, and even then there is a 15 minute wait.
