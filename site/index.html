Questions

Plain facts.

What if I lose the key?

Pair a new key in the Pawl app. It takes over after a wait of at least 15 minutes, so a spare key is never a shortcut.

How long do apps stay open?

30 minutes by default, then they block again. You can change that time in the app.

Do I need an account?

No account and no tracking. Pawl does not collect what you block or browse. The code is public.

Shipping and returns

Kits ship to US addresses within 3 business days. Full refund within 30 days if the key is unopened.
