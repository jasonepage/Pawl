Pawl: Commitment Lock

Block gambling apps. Keep the key out of reach.

Pawl uses Apple Screen Time to block the apps and sites you choose. There is no unlock button. To unblock, you need a physical security key you have left somewhere else, and then a 15 minute wait.

Read the source on GitHub

Free on iPhone and iPad. Blocking, the key, the wait and your streak are free. Linking a sponsor is a paid add-on. You will need a USB-C security key (FIDO2), sold separately.

How it works

Three steps turn a quick tap into a choice you have to mean.

1

Pick what to block

Choose sportsbook, casino and other gambling apps and sites, or anything else you keep reaching for. Apple Screen Time does the blocking and shows a calm locked screen with no unlock button.

2

Put the key somewhere else

Unblocking needs a physical USB-C security key. Leave it at a friend's place, in your car, or anywhere that is not your pocket at 1am.

3

Wait it out

Even with the key in hand, unblocking starts a 15 minute wait. After a short open window (30 minutes by default), the block comes back on by itself.

The key is the whole trick

Most blockers fail at 1am because you can switch them off with one tap. Pawl moves that decision to a small object you have already put out of reach.

Plug it in once

When you set Pawl up, you plug a USB-C security key into your phone and tap it. Pawl remembers that key and only that key.

Leave it somewhere else

Give it to a friend, put it in your car, or lock it in a drawer at work. The further away it is, the longer the urge has to fade.

The 1am version of you has to go get it

Even when you do get the key, tapping it starts a 15 minute wait. Then the block lifts for a short window (30 minutes by default) and comes back on by itself.

Which key to buy: any FIDO2 security key with a USB-C plug. Several makers sell them, and they need no app or account. Pawl does not sell keys and does not take a cut.

Private by design

Screen Time hands Pawl only anonymous tokens. Pawl never learns which apps or sites you picked.

Your clean day streak lives on your phone and in your own iCloud, so it survives deleting the app. No advertising and no tracking. Signing in and adding a sponsor are optional, and you can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.
