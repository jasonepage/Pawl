01 / Why Pawl

You cannot willpower your way through a button designed to be tapped.

Other blockers leave their off switch on the same phone as the temptation. Pawl moves that decision into a physical key. Put it in the garage, at work, or with someone you trust. Your impulsive self cannot tap through what is not there.

02 / How it works

Three deliberate steps.
No button to bargain with.

1

Choose what to block

Pick the apps and sites you keep opening. Apple Screen Time does the blocking.

2

Move the decision away

Keep the key out of reach, or let a trusted person approve from their iPhone with Pawl Pro.

3

Wait 15 minutes

The key or your trusted person's approval starts the wait. It never skips. Your blocks return after the open window.

03 / In the moment

The urge can be immediate.
The answer does not have to be.

At the moment you would usually tap through, Pawl gives you time instead. There is no instant unlock inside the blocked app.

Key or trusted person→15 minute wait→Short open window
