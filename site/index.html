Pawl: App Blocker With a Key

Block any app. Keep the key out of reach.

Pawl uses Apple Screen Time to block the apps and sites you choose. Nothing on the phone can lift the block. To unblock, you need a physical security key you have left somewhere else, and then a 15 minute wait.

Read the source on GitHub

Blocking, the key, the wait and your streak are free. Pawl Pro is optional ($3.99 a month or $29.99 a year in the US, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan) and adds new scheduled sets and the full weekly report. You need a USB-C security key (FIDO2), sold separately.

How it works

Most blockers fail at 1am because you can switch them off with one tap. Pawl moves that decision to a small object you have already put out of reach.

1

Pick what to block

Choose any apps and sites you keep reaching for: betting, adult sites, social feeds, games, or the one app that eats your evenings. Apple Screen Time does the blocking and shows a calm locked screen with no unlock button.

2

Put the key somewhere else

Unblocking needs a physical USB-C security key. You plug it in once when you set Pawl up, and Pawl remembers that key and only that key. Then leave it at a friend's place, in your car, or anywhere that is not your pocket at 1am.

3

Wait it out

Even with the key in hand, unblocking starts a 15 minute wait. After a short open window (30 minutes by default), the block comes back on by itself.
