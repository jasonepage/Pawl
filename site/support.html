Support

Pawl helps you quit something you're struggling with, such as gambling, adult content, or any distracting app, by blocking it with Apple's Screen Time and making unblocking deliberately hard. Here's help with the most common questions. Can't find what you need? Email support@getpawl.com.

How does Pawl work?

You choose what to block. Pawl shields it with Apple's Screen Time and shows a calm “Locked by Pawl” screen in its place. Unblocking requires a physical USB-C security key (FIDO2, bought separately) you've put out of reach, plus a 15-minute cooling-off timer, so the impulsive choice becomes a deliberate one.

Do I need an account?

No. The blocker works fully without signing in. Signing in with Apple is optional and only enables the sponsor feature: a trusted person who approves your unlock requests and is alerted if your protection is removed.

How do I delete my account?

Open Pawl → Settings → Account → Delete account → confirm. This permanently deletes your account and all cloud data (profile, sponsor link, history, devices, alerts) and can't be undone. If you can't access the app, email privacy@getpawl.com from your account email and we'll delete it for you.

How do I remove Pawl from my phone?

While Pawl is actively protecting you, it intentionally resists being deleted by long-pressing its icon. To remove it completely: open Settings → Screen Time, turn off Screen Time (or remove Pawl under “Apps with Screen Time Access”), then delete Pawl from your Home Screen.

What security key do I need?

Any FIDO2 security key with a USB-C plug works. Several makers sell them, and they need no app or account. You buy the key separately; Pawl does not sell keys and does not take a cut.

Is Pawl free?

Yes. Blocking, the security key, the cooling-off wait, your streak, and the crisis tools are free and stay free. Pawl Pro is an optional subscription that adds a sponsor: a trusted person who approves your unlock requests and is alerted if your protection drops. You can restore a subscription from Settings inside the app.
